General information on data processing

The protection of your personal data is of particular concern to us. We process your data exclusively on the basis of the statutory provisions, in particular the GDPR and the relevant Austrian regulations.

Contacting us

If you contact us via the form on the website or by email, the data you provide will be processed in order to handle your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions.

The legal basis is generally the performance of pre-contractual measures or our legitimate interest in efficient communication.

Contact form

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message – and, if you provide them, your company and phone number – are transmitted to our server and forwarded to us by email. We also record the language of the form and the time at which you confirmed the privacy policy.

Name, email address and message are required so that we can answer your enquiry. By submitting the form you consent to the processing of your details for this purpose. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example by email to office@stolz-consulting.net.

To protect against automated requests, the form contains a field that is invisible to people (honeypot). No cookies and no external services are used for this.

If sending via the server is not possible, your email program opens with the prepared message. It is only sent once you send it there.

Server log files

When you visit this website, connection data such as your IP address, time of access, page visited and browser information may be processed for technical reasons. This processing serves the technical security and stable operation of the website.

Fonts (hosted locally)

The fonts used on this website (Inter and Source Serif 4) are delivered locally from our own server. No connection is made to servers of Google or other third parties; only the connection data that arises anyway when a page is accessed is generated on our own server (see server log files).

Storage period

Personal data is only stored for as long as is necessary for the respective purposes or as required by statutory retention obligations.

Your rights

In principle, you have the right to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, revocation and objection. If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
