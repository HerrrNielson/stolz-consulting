Services

Services at a glance

Controlling, financing, internationalisation, ERP-related IT and training – five service areas for small and medium-sized companies, matched to your question.

Short-term or long-term

Some questions can be settled in a short, clearly defined engagement; others call for longer support. Both are possible – the scope follows your question.

Controlling and financial managementPlanning, management, reporting and economic development.

Planning, management and reporting become transparent – as a basis for decisions and for the economic development of your company.

When this helps

  • Planning and reporting should become clearer and more reliable.
  • A controlling strategy is to be set up or developed further.
  • Liquidity and key figures are to be monitored systematically.
FinancingTailor-made financing for your project.

Financing that fits your company’s situation and plans and is built on transparent planning.

When this helps

  • An investment or a growth step is to be financed.
  • Existing financing is to be reviewed in light of current planning.
  • Liquidity planning is to be reliable for the next steps.
Internationalisation and growth supportSupport with international challenges, expansion and organisational development.

Support with international questions, expansion and organisational development – drawing on experience abroad in Russia, the Baltic states, China, the USA, France and Germany.

When this helps

  • A step into new markets or abroad is coming up.
  • Growth raises new questions for organisation and reporting.
  • The environment is international and calls for experience with foreign markets.
